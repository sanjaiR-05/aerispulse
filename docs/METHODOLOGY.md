## 2. Atmospheric Inhaled Particulate Matter Dosage Model

### Formulation
The human daily inhaled particulate matter dosage ($D$, in micrograms per day, $\mu\text{g/day}$) is modeled as a function of environmental pollutant concentration, activity-specific minute ventilation rates, exposure durations, and personal filtration factors:

$$D = \sum_{j \in \{\text{commute}, \text{indoor}, \text{outdoor}\}} C_j \times V_{E, j} \times t_j \times (1 - \eta_{\text{mask}, j}) \times (1 - \eta_{\text{filter}, j})$$

Where:
- $C_j$: Micro-environmental pollutant concentration ($\mu\text{g/m}^3$) for activity or zone $j$.
- $V_{E, j}$: Human minute ventilation rate ($\text{m}^3/\text{hour}$).
  - Resting / Indoor Sedentary: $0.48\,\text{m}^3/\text{h}$ ($8.0\,\text{L/min}$).
  - Low-intensity Commute (Car / Metro): $0.60\,\text{m}^3/\text{h}$ ($10.0\,\text{L/min}$).
  - Moderate Commute (Two-wheeler / Scooter): $0.90\,\text{m}^3/\text{h}$ ($15.0\,\text{L/min}$).
  - Active Commute (Bicycle / Walking): $1.80\,\text{m}^3/\text{h}$ ($30.0\,\text{L/min}$).
  - High-Ventilation Exercise (Outdoor Cardio / Running): $3.00\,\text{m}^3/\text{h}$ ($50.0\,\text{L/min}$).
- $t_j$: Exposure duration in hours per day.
- $\eta_{\text{mask}}$: Particulate filtration efficiency of personal protective equipment:
  - None: $\eta = 0.00$ ($0\%$).
  - Cloth / Basic Surgical: $\eta = 0.40$ ($40\%$).
  - N95 / FFP2 / KN95 Respirator (well-fitted): $\eta = 0.95$ ($95\%$).
- $\eta_{\text{filter}}$: Micro-environment indoor air purification filtration factor:
  - Ambient unfiltered indoor infiltration ratio: $I/O \approx 0.70$ ($\eta_{\text{filter}} = 0.30$).
  - True HEPA Air Purifier (CADR compliant): $\eta_{\text{filter}} = 0.85$ ($85\%$ ambient particle clearance).

### Scientific Benchmark Citations
1. **World Health Organization (WHO):** *WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines: Particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10), ozone, nitrogen dioxide, sulfur dioxide and carbon monoxide.* World Health Organization, Geneva, 2021.
2. **Burnett, R., et al.:** *Global estimates of mortality associated with long-term exposure to outdoor fine particulate matter.* Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS), 115(38), 9592-9597, 2018.

## 3. United States Environmental Protection Agency (US EPA) AQI Formulation

The sub-index for any pollutant ($I_p$) is calculated using the standard piecewise linear interpolation formula:

$$I_p = \frac{I_{\text{high}} - I_{\text{low}}}{C_{\text{high}} - C_{\text{low}}} (C_p - C_{\text{low}}) + I_{\text{low}}$$

Where:
- $C_p$: The truncated concentration of pollutant $p$.
- $C_{\text{low}}, C_{\text{high}}$: The concentration breakpoints corresponding to $C_p$.
- $I_{\text{low}}, I_{\text{high}}$: The index breakpoints corresponding to $C_{\text{low}}$ and $C_{\text{high}}$.

### US EPA PM2.5 Breakpoints ($\mu\text{g/m}^3$):
| Category | AQI Range | $C_{\text{low}} - C_{\text{high}}$ |
|---|---|---|
| Good | 0 – 50 | 0.0 – 9.0 |
| Moderate | 51 – 100 | 9.1 – 35.4 |
| Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups | 101 – 150 | 35.5 – 55.4 |
| Unhealthy | 151 – 200 | 55.5 – 125.4 |
| Very Unhealthy | 201 – 300 | 125.5 – 225.4 |
| Hazardous | 301 – 500 | 225.5 – 325.4 |

- **United States Environmental Protection Agency (US EPA):** *Technical Assistance Document for the Reporting of Daily Air Quality – the Air Quality Index (AQI).* EPA-454/B-18-007, 2018.

## 4. Carbon Abatement Modeling (DEFRA 2023 Factors)

Daily carbon emissions abated ($\Delta E_{\text{CO}_2}$, in $\text{kg CO}_2/\text{day}$) when a user shifts from private internal combustion engine (ICE) transit to low-carbon modalities is calculated as:

$$\Delta E_{\text{CO}_2} = d_{\text{commute}} \times (EF_{\text{baseline}} - EF_{\text{chosen}})$$

Where $d_{\text{commute}}$ is the round-trip distance in kilometers ($\text{km}$), and $EF$ represents the greenhouse gas emission factor ($\text{kg CO}_2\text{e/passenger-km}$):
- Baseline (Single-Occupancy ICE Car): $EF_{\text{ICE}} = 0.170\,\text{kg CO}_2/\text{km}$.
- Two-Wheeler / Petrol Scooter: $EF_{\text{2W}} = 0.065\,\text{kg CO}_2/\text{km}$.
- Electric Bus / Metro Rail (calibrated electric rapid transit): $EF_{\text{transit}} = 0.006\,\text{kg CO}_2/\text{km}$.
- Walking / Bicycle: $EF_{\text{active}} = 0.000\,\text{kg CO}_2/\text{km}$.

These four intensities are declared exactly once in `src/domain/constants.ts` (`CARBON_EMISSION_FACTORS`) and consumed directly by `carbon-calculator.ts`, so the documented table and the computed output cannot diverge.

### Scientific Citation
- **UK Department for Environment, Food & Rural Affairs (DEFRA):** *Government greenhouse gas conversion factors for company reporting.* 2023.

## 5. Economic & Outpatient Health Savings Metric

The estimated monthly outpatient medical and productivity savings ($\Delta S_{\text{INR}}$, in Indian Rupees per month) is derived from epidemiological concentration-response functions for urban Indian metropolitan zones:

$$\Delta S_{\text{INR}} = \text{round}_{10}\left( \frac{D_{\text{baseline}} - D_{\text{mitigated}}}{D_{\text{baseline}}} \times S_{\text{base\_factor}} \right)$$

Where:
- $S_{\text{base\_factor}} = ₹2,200/\text{month}$, reflecting baseline average monthly per-capita outpatient medication, inhaler expenditure, and avoided lost productive days during severe pollution months in Indian Tier-1 cities.
- When exposure reduction is $0\%$, $\Delta S = ₹0$.
- Under maximum mitigation (N95 respirator + HEPA purifier + low-exposure transit), monthly savings reach up to $₹1,800 - ₹2,500/\text{month}$.

## 6. Personal Health Shield Score (0 – 100 Index)

The Personal Health Shield Score ($S_{\text{shield}}$) computes an individual's overall physiological defense level based on their current environmental exposure and active mitigation controls:

The model is additive: a baseline floor of 10 points is raised by each active mitigation and then clamped to $[0, 100]$:

$$S_{\text{shield}} = \text{clamp}_{0,100}\left( 10 + w_{\text{mask}} + w_{\text{hepa}} + w_{\text{transit}} - p_{\text{cardio}} \right)$$

Where (all declared in `SHIELD_WEIGHTS`, `src/domain/constants.ts`):
- $w_{\text{mask}}$: N95/FFP2 $= 45$, surgical/cloth $= 20$, none $= 0$.
- $w_{\text{hepa}}$: active True HEPA recirculation $= 40$, otherwise $0$.
- $w_{\text{transit}}$: sealed metro / electric bus $= 3$, otherwise $0$.
- $p_{\text{cardio}} = 5 \times t_{\text{cardio}}$ (hours), applied only when $AQI > 150$, cardio exceeds one hour, and no N95 is worn. The floor after penalty is 5 points.
- Ambient floors: $AQI \le 50$ raises the score to at least 85; $AQI \le 100$ raises it to at least 65.

Consequences:
- If no protective measures are taken in high AQI, the score collapses to $10/100$ (as demonstrated in the baseline state).
- When a user enacts an N95 respirator, active indoor HEPA filtration, and sheltered metro transit, the score rises to $98/100$; clean ambient air can lift it to $100/100$.
